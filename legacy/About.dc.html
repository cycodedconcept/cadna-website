Why we exist

A business owner in Lagos looking to expand needs a lawyer, a strategist, a bank, a developer and a trainer. Five relationships, five briefings, five invoices — and nobody accountable for the whole outcome.

CADNA operates as a concierge. You submit one request. We diagnose it, route it to the right division, assign an owner, and stay accountable through delivery. Our four divisions — business, finance, education and technology — exist because real problems rarely sit inside just one.

We work across six countries, and every engagement begins the same way: a complimentary diagnostic, an honest assessment, and a written plan with pricing before any commitment.
